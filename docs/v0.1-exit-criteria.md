# v0.1 exit criteria: measured results

Final measurement on 2026-11-08, after the review fixes, against the `feat/v0.1` tree. The stack ran as
the real image (`drumsergio/ownhealth:0.1.0-dev`, arm64, 582 MB) under `docker compose` with a fresh
`postgres:17.11` and fresh volumes, on a Mac mini (10 cores, 16 GB; the Docker VM had 6 CPUs and 8 GB).
All data is synthetic: the Rivera family and the fictional LabNorte lab.

- **Fresh install**: an empty database, migrated by the one-shot `admin` service as the owner role; the
  app connects as `ownhealth_app`. The owner was created with a passkey through the first-run screen,
  driven by Chromium's virtual WebAuthn authenticator. Then the synthetic 3-page LabNorte PDF, an Apple
  Health `export.zip` (90 days), the synthetic IPS bundle and Alex's `genome-facts.json` with its VCF and
  gVCF were uploaded over REST with the session, and four PATs were exercised with the Python MCP SDK over
  streamable HTTP.
- **Demo Alex** (82 lab values from 2021-11-09 to 2026-09-14) was measured by the test suite on the same
  tree (`tests/mcp/test_mcp_budget.py`).

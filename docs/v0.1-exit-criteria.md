# v0.1 exit criteria: measured results

Measured on 2026-11-08 against the integrated `feat/v0.1` tree. The stack ran as the real image
(`drumsergio/ownhealth:0.1.0-dev`, arm64, 581 MB) under `docker compose` with a fresh `postgres:17.11`,
on a Mac mini (10 cores, 16 GB; the Docker VM had 6 CPUs and 8 GB). All data is synthetic: the Rivera
family and the fictional LabNorte lab.

Two stacks were used:

- **Fresh install**: an empty database. The owner was created with a passkey through the first-run screen,
  driven by Chromium's virtual WebAuthn authenticator. Then the synthetic 3-page LabNorte PDF, an Apple
  Health `export.zip` (90 days), the synthetic IPS bundle and Alex's `genome-facts.json` with its VCF and
  gVCF were uploaded over REST, and four PATs were exercised with the Python MCP SDK over streamable HTTP.
- **Demo**: `OWNHEALTH_DEMO=1`, where Alex has 82 lab values from 2021-11-09 to 2026-09-14.

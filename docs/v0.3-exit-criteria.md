# v0.3 exit criteria: measured results

Measured on 2026-11-19 against the tree that ships as 0.4.0 (main at 36c1c3e plus the version bump), on a Mac mini (10 cores, 16 GB) with Postgres 17.11 in Docker. Each criterion has automated tests that pass; the numbers below are what those tests printed, what the shipped goldens hold, or what a demo instance of the same build answered. `cargo test --workspace --locked` passed with 1336 tests, 0 failed, and the contract suite with 79. Seven criteria are met. The status budget is not met by 1 byte for one demo page in Spanish. All data is synthetic: the Rivera family, Carmen Ortega and the fictional LabNorte lab.

## Upgrade from 0.3.0

Each new migration has an upgrade test on a 0.3.0 database (the 0.3.0 schema with the 0.3.0 demo seed): `ownhealth-rules` `medication_lane` (1100) and `supplement_decision` (1101); `ownhealth-ops` `family_upgrade` (1110), `family_share_upgrade` (1111), `prevention_upgrade` (1130) and `calendar_feed_upgrade` (1131); `ownhealth` `clinvar_upgrade` (1120). Every profile keeps its `state_hash` across the migrations. Rule pack 0.2.0 then moves every `state_hash` once, on purpose; the pins are in `ownhealth-rules/tests/fixtures/expected.json`.

## Known gaps

- Page 1 of Alex's demo status is 2049 bytes with a Spanish token, 1 byte over the 2 KB budget (the Status budget row).
- The demo shows no family hint and no computed SCORE2 or PREVENT. No relative has a high LDL with a share, and Alex's draw has no HDL, pressure or smoking status. The tests above seed their own cases.
- A lab value a final report confirmed is exported as final, but its stored FHIR copy still says `preliminary`.
- The doctor summary reads its parts in separate statements at the default isolation, not in one snapshot.
- The rule pack's own defaults (recency windows for thyroid, liver, blood count, glucose and others) cite our design doc, marked `basis: pack_default`.
